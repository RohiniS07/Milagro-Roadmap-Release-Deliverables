Report Verification — Darshan

Track C · Backend · Parallel
D
Darshan is responsible for all backend scripting and Playwright test cases for SmartPOS report verification. For every transaction completed in Track B, Darshan validates that Revenue Summary, Sales Revenue and Payment Summary remain consistent between Open Day and Closed Day states across 3 dedicated test locations. This track runs in parallel via API work.
📍 3 Test Locations
Location 1 — Cash Discount enabled (3.85%)
Location 2 — Cash Discount disabled
Location 3 — Beverage + Print Ingredients configuration
Each location validated: smt_location, user, POS device, menu, payment settings, pos_day_management, receipt templates
📊 Reports Verified
Revenue Summary — Gross Sales, Net Sales, Tax, Total Revenue
Sales Revenue — by category (Food, Beverage etc.)
Payment Summary — Cash, CC, Gift Card totals
Open Day captured before End Day → compared with Closed Day after processing
💳 Transaction Combinations
Cash · Credit Card · Gift Card payments
Promo · Loyalty · Service Charge · Suggested Tip
Manual/Add Tip · Discount + Service Charge
Cash Discount scenario · Non-Cash Discount scenario
⚖️ Comparison Rules
Strict match: Gross Sales, Net Sales, Tax, Promos, Loyalty, Tips, GC values, Payment totals
Warning only: Cash Deposit, Closing Cash, Ending Cash
Normalized decimal comparison — ignore $, commas
Fail if row missing or amount/count mismatch found
